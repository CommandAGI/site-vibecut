Why this cut

    The cut is a plain JSON file: every shot, where it starts, which second of the source it uses. Render a video to keep the picture; it records as it plays, in real time.

    How it works

    One description becomes an edit decision list: which shot, from which second, for how long, in which order. You see the list as a timeline and in a file.

    1. Describe itLength in seconds or minutes, kinds of shot, pace, the subjects you want, and what to leave out. English, Spanish or Chinese.
    2. It cutsThe engine matches your words to the footage, keeps the length you asked for to the millisecond, and ends on the card unless you rule it out.
    3. Keep itScrub the timeline, play the cut, download the cut as JSON, or render it to a video file in the browser.

    What you get

    A first version, built to be used today. The sample footage is a studio; your own footage and a longer render are the next release.

    A cut you can read

    Every decision is a file: shots, in and out points, speed, and the reasons for each choice. No account, no waiting on a queue.

    The reasons, in words

    VibeCut says why it left a shot out or ended on the card, so a cut is a conversation, not a black box.

    In your browser

    The engine, the footage and the playback run on the page. Nothing is uploaded and nothing is tracked.

    Three languages

    English, Spanish and Simplified Chinese, on the same site, with the A/文 picker.
